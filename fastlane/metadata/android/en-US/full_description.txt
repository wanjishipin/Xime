Xime (曦码) is a Chinese IME built on the Rime engine for Wubi and Pinyin input, focused on a simple and efficient typing experience.

Features:
* Multiple input schemes - Wubi 86, Wubi 98, Pinyin, and mixed Wubi/Pinyin
* Rime engine - mature and stable Rime input method engine
* Speech-to-text - built-in voice recognition (FunAsr and local models)
* Emoji plugins - supports extendable emoji plugins
* Material Design 3 style with light/dark themes
